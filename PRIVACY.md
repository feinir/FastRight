# FastRight Privacy Policy / FastRight 隐私政策

Effective November 24, 2026 / 生效日期 2026 年 11 月 24 日

## English

FastRight performs Finder and menu bar operations on your Mac. FastRight does not require an account, use advertising or analytics SDKs, track users, or send your files, settings, or usage data to the developer.

FastRight stores your menu configuration, templates, favorites, and folder authorizations locally. The Mac App Store version shares these settings with its bundled Finder extension. When you choose an operation, FastRight accesses the relevant files and folders. Copy and cut use the macOS clipboard. When you choose to open an item in another application, FastRight passes the item to the application you selected.

Settings and saved authorizations remain on your Mac until you remove them. You can revoke access to a folder in FastRight’s General settings, and disable the Finder extension or revoke Finder automation access in macOS System Settings. If you want to remove all stored preferences, uninstall FastRight and remove its remaining local application data. FastRight keeps no server-side copy.

For privacy questions, use the project’s support page. [FastRight support](https://github.com/feinir/FastRight/issues)

## 简体中文

FastRight 在你的 Mac 上执行访达和菜单栏操作。FastRight 无需注册账号，不使用广告或分析 SDK，不追踪用户，也不会把文件、设置或使用数据发送给开发者。

菜单配置、模板、收藏和目录授权保存在本机。Mac App Store 版与内置的访达扩展共享这些设置。只有在执行相关操作时，FastRight 才访问对应的文件和目录。「复制」和「剪切」使用 macOS 剪贴板。选择用其他软件打开项目时，FastRight 会将项目交给所选软件。

设置和授权保留在本机，直到你移除它们。可在 FastRight 的「通用偏好」撤销单个目录授权，也可在 macOS「系统设置」关闭访达扩展或撤销访达自动化权限。要清除全部配置，可卸载 FastRight 并删除其本地应用数据。开发者没有服务器端副本。

隐私问题可通过项目支持页联系。 [FastRight 支持](https://github.com/feinir/FastRight/issues)
